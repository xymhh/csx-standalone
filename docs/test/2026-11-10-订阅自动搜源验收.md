## 2. 接口实测（本地 TestClient）

- `GET /api/v1/auto_search/settings` → `{enabled:false, interval_hours:6, auto_transfer:true, max_per_round:10, per_subscription_limit:20}`
- `PUT /api/v1/auto_search/settings` → 返回合并后的设置
- `POST /api/v1/auto_search/run {subscription_id}` → `{candidates, tasks, skipped, sources_ok, elapsed_ms}`
